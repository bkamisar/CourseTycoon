Course Tycoon: what's new in Act III

Build 2026-10-04a. Notes for playtesters.

Act III (the championships) got a big rework. It used to be one day you didn't do anything on. Now it's a three-week build-up and a week of rounds with decisions in all of it.

The big picture

  • Three rungs, in order: County Open (2 rounds), Regional (3), National (4). The course is shut to the public for every round.
  • Each morning of the week you set the pins (easy / fair / tough / brutal) on the Championship sheet. You'll see the leader, the score the winner is on pace for, the target, and today's weather beside them.
  • You can watch the field play your course on the playback.
  • The week is judged on the winning score. It has to land in a range. Too low (the field took the course apart) fails just as too high does.
  • A minimum course difficulty is checked on the first morning. Narrower fairways are the cheap way to get there. The course is locked once round 1 starts.
  • Miss it and you get the base fee only, lose reputation, and the rung goes to someone else for 120 days. Then you can bid again.

The run-up (the three weeks before)

  • Practice round, 11 days out: the officials play your course and tell you where the winner is likely to finish, as a range. It's a forecast, not a promise, and it tells you in time to fix things.
  • Weather matters to the crew: drizzle slows them, heavy rain slows them a lot and softens a firm course, and a storm stops work. Read the forecast.
  • Championship events: a mower dies, a sponsor wants the 18th tee, a green floods, someone complains about a pin. Every championship gets at least one, and week events change how the next round plays. Each choice states its cost.
  • Staffing: a championship wants about eight groundskeepers. Keeping the course firm costs money every day of the run-up, and the turf can wear out during the week.

Money

Hosting well roughly pays for itself. It is no longer a money-maker, and a missed week costs real money. The reward is reputation and finishing the act.
  • Best-case purses: County $148k, Regional $352k, National $740k.
  • The National's crowd bonus is now earnable with cheap hotel amenities (the short course isn't needed).

Also fixed

  • The bottom toolbar scrolls on small phones, so "Open the day" is always reachable.
  • The starter no longer talks about public golfers on a closed day.
  • Hole closures from decision events can't sink a championship; it's always played over the full open course.
  • An old save that missed its National gets it back.

Please tell me about

  • Does the practice round's forecast feel honest by round 1?
  • Does the week feel like it has enough to do each morning, or too much?
  • Any event card where the costs, or the effect on the next round, don't match what it says.
  • Any number that disagrees between the Championship sheet, the HUD line and the evening report.
  • The Regional and National: can you land the winner in range, and is it fun when you can't?

Known limits: the National is a bit of a gamble even when played well (about 3 in 4), and the practice round assumes normal weather.
